# Mímir 0.10

This version makes the phone's conversations easier to follow and the same everywhere: one reply box, a bubble around every answer, times and read receipts on every message, and a Recent activity list.

- One reply box on every screen: the words on top, then + on the left, one model and effort chip in the middle, and a small Send on the right. The project page now starts a task with the model and effort you pick. Voice typing is in each screen's three dots (a Whisper keeps its mic in the box).
- The task screen's top is one slim row: the state, what it is doing right now, a small round Stop and the three dots. Find and voice typing are in the three dots.
- Every answer sits in its own grey bubble, the mirror of your blue one.
- Under each answer, one short row: a small Listen button, then the model and effort that really answered and the time. Copy and Share are in the press-and-hold menu.
- Times on every message, and read receipts on yours: an empty circle when it is sent, a circle with a check when your PC has handed it to the AI, a filled circle when the AI has read it. If a message is not read after a minute, a line says why.
- When the model changes between answers, its name turns amber with the reason ("usage limit", "you picked it", or "reason not reported" when none was given). Tap it for more.
- While the AI works, three dots move on its side of the chat until the answer begins.
- Recent activity takes the place of Updates in the side menu: every chat and project task, newest first, in two-line bubbles under Today and Earlier. Spoken updates are one tap away at the top of that page. Whisper moves up under New chat.
- A new conversation in a project is named by the time it started ("Nov 25, 5:50 PM").
- A project's checks are one slim line; the full list is in the three dots.
- Your PC shows the processor and graphics temperature first on their lines.
- Fixed: messages sent to a task while it was doing background work could be lost (found by you); a picture's file path no longer shows in your bubble.

Known not working yet: the PC window doesn't have the new times, receipts and Listen row yet (the phone does). Older messages have no times, because times were not saved before this version. The project separator (at most two projects in development at once) comes in the next version. Not measured on a real phone yet: how smooth the moving dots are.
