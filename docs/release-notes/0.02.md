# Mímir 0.02

The first public release. Private by design.

- Ask anything from your phone, tablet or PC. Your chats stay on your devices.
- Pair your phone with your PC once, at the PC, and use it from anywhere.
- One PC, several people: each person's phone sees only their own chats.
- Choose who answers: your PC's free model, a model on the phone, or Online (uses your Anthropic plan; your messages go to Anthropic).
- Honest answers: Mímir says when it isn't sure, gives the main sides on disputed questions, and shows its sources when it looks things up.
- Edit an earlier message and carry on from there.
- Lock Mímir with your fingerprint, face or phone PIN (off unless you turn it on).
- Updates come from this page and are checked before they install: the phone updates itself, and the PC updates from its own Flatpak source.
- Labs: check that everything works, and report a problem in a few taps (nothing is sent unless you send it).
