# Mímir 0.03

- Send photos and screenshots from your phone: your PC looks at them (no download on the phone). Hidden details like location are removed first.
- Send PDFs, documents, spreadsheets, slides, e-books and zip files: your PC reads them and says how much it read.
- Paste or drop pictures into the chat on your PC.
- Nothing is lost when your PC is away: messages wait and send themselves when it's back, and an answer cut off by a dropped connection is finished.
- Every problem now comes with a next step (Try again, Answer without looking it up, Open settings).
- Smoother menu and settings; turning a tablet keeps your place.
- Fixes for people sharing one PC, updates and the PC's background part.
