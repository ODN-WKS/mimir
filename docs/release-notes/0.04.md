# Mímir 0.04

- Photos always get an answer: if your usual model can't look at pictures, another model on your PC does (or online, if you allow it), and a small line says which one answered and why.
- A brand-new PC answers its first message straight away, even while it's busy.
- Right-click Cut, Copy and Paste in every place you type on the PC; right-click Paste also takes a copied picture into the message box.
- Each chat keeps its own unsent words and pictures.
- Pictures stay in the box when nothing can look at them right now, with a note; nothing is lost.
- A failed web lookup shows one Try again and says what really went wrong.
- Tablets keep the menu open on the left when held upright too.
- Clearer messages: the camera and microphone notes fit narrow phones, a failed update check says one plain thing, and the update button comes back after a failed or cancelled install.
- On the PC: the pairing card starts fresh for each phone, and a failed update says why with Try again.
- Someone else's phone no longer shows your PC's Rename or "reach from anywhere" settings.
- Phones no longer offer "pair another phone": new phones are paired from Mímir on the PC.
- The app no longer asks to draw over other apps.

Known not working yet: smoother motion on older phones (next version); the startup eye was not checked by eye on a real screen; online answers with pictures were tested only in automatic tests.
