# Mímir 0.08

This version is about using Mímir for real work from the phone.

- Always a sign of life: from the moment you tap Send, the task shows what's happening, in plain words with a running time: "Sending…", "Your PC got it", "Thinking…", then the step ("Reading a file", "Running a command", "Waiting for your OK"). If your PC goes quiet for a minute, it says so instead of looking stuck, and it says when the connection is down. The PC window shows the same line.
- Photos and files in task replies: attach from the camera, your photos or files (or paste), even with no words; they lose their hidden details on your PC like everything else. A reply sent while your PC is away keeps its pictures until it can go.
- Model and effort for each task reply, from the phone.
- Your PC's own tools in Mímir's sessions: work started from Mímir runs on your PC with your normal tools.
- Stop for read-aloud: "Stop reading" while your PC's voice reads, and taps never queue up.
- The PC window's right side is open from the start, with your PC's processor, memory and graphics at the very top, as in the old app.
- Quieter PC: no desktop pop-up when a task finishes unless you switch it on (Needs you still tells you).
- Stop on the PC now ends Claude Code and everything it started, also when Mímir runs as the Flatpak.
- A picture is never dropped quietly: if it can't be read or your PC refuses the reply, the reply stays with Try again and Remove; chosen pictures stay when you rotate the phone or leave the screen.
- Private file names (keys, passwords, hidden files) show only as "Reading a file".
- Fixes from the 0.07 checks: a session running in another app can't be replied to twice; a task whose helper crashed says so at once; and more.

Known not working yet: while you scroll back through a session running in another app, a new line jumps you to the bottom; if read-aloud can't start, the reason shows in the chat list, not the task; Settings is not reorganized yet (next); the phone and PC window don't yet have every feature of each other (a full side-by-side list comes next); a few small wording and layout points from the checks are listed for 0.09.
