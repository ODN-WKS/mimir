# Mímir 0.09

This version fixes what got in the way on the phone: things you couldn't find, a New chat that wasn't new, answers that didn't say who wrote them, and look-ups that never happened.

- New chat is always empty. A message still waiting to be sent stays in the chat you wrote it in, and its reply lands there.
- Every answer says who wrote it ("This phone", "Your PC" or Claude, with the model), and the top of the chat says when this phone is answering because your PC can't, and until when.
- Look-ups that work: questions about products, ratings and prices search the web; if the encyclopedia has nothing, the web is searched instead. Private questions ("for me", "I have...") are not sent to a search. The models are told that Mímir looks things up for them, so they no longer claim they can't.
- Your PC's own model starts when a chat needs it, instead of the phone quietly answering.
- Stop sits at the top right of a task and asks "Stop?" first. In Settings › Look and size you can choose "Hold 2 seconds", "Confirm" or "Off". This also covers the phone's own chat.
- A task has a menu button at the top right: Archive, Refresh, Delete, Rename, Move, Change the model.
- On a project page, hold or swipe a task to archive or delete it, with Undo; pull down to refresh. Deleting a task removes its conversation and files from your PC too.
- Back from a task goes to where you came from; a second Back always reaches the first page.
- A question from a task is one slim line above the reply box, quoting the question; it goes away when you reply.
- Files you attach show by name, never as a path on your PC.
- The keyboard opens by itself for New chat and other boxes that need typing (switch in Settings › Look and size).
- Your PC's processor, memory and graphics on the phone (Your PC screen).
- Claude use left this week shows on the chat screen and in the side menu. Your PC reads it only while Mímir is really using Claude; when there's no number, it says why.
- Quieter by default: only what needs you sends a notification. Switches you changed yourself stay as you set them.

Known not working yet: Settings is not reorganized yet; the phone and PC window don't yet have every feature of each other; a few items from the 0.09 list come in the next update (the snappiness check on a real phone, the method research, the switch-over from the old app). Not yet tried on a real phone: the top of the chat when this phone takes over from your PC's model, and a real Claude use number.
