# Mímir 0.05

- Your phone now tells you when a project task finishes or needs you, within seconds, even while the phone sleeps. While a task works, Mímir keeps its own private link to your PC open (a quiet notice shows), and closes it a couple of minutes after the work ends. No outside push service is used, and the alert never shows what the task was doing.
- Stop is always on screen beside Send while a task works, and it really stops: Mímir and anything it started end within seconds, also during a permission question and after a dropped link.
- Honest status: a task that asks you something says "Needs you", a task still running something in the background says Working (with Stop), and Done only shows when nothing is running.
- A new task started while your PC is away waits on the phone ("Waiting for your PC") and starts by itself, once, when the PC is back. You can cancel it while it waits, and if the PC can't start it, your words are kept with Try again.
- "New project…" in the side menu, under Projects.
- On the PC: pictures and files can go into project tasks, and every picture is redrawn clean (no location or camera details) before the model sees it.
- A new icon: a golden-bronze closed eye.
- Security: the PDF reader is updated (a specially made PDF could crash the PC service), and a few unused parts were removed.

Known not working yet: project task screens on the phone still lack Copy, Edit, Try again, Share, Read aloud and attaching (next version); answers don't draw tables yet; smoother motion on older phones; battery use of the new alerts was not measured on a phone running on battery.
