# Mímir 0.11

This version puts everything fixed since 0.10 together: a status card right in the chat, a press-and-hold menu on the phone, a Skill choice beside Model and Effort, Fresh start when a conversation runs long, the PC's own model taking over when online use runs out, and chats that keep answering after a quiet spell.

- Type "status" in a chat and a card shows there: what runs on your PC, since when, how far along, the next step and what needs you. No model is used for it.
- Press and hold a project, chat or task on the phone to move, fold, archive or delete it forever. A phone can delete a project for good: its tasks and their conversations go with it, and the folder on your PC stays.
- A Skill bubble sits beside Model and Effort: pick one of seven skills for the next message. It shows its whole name, and it moves to its own line when there is too little room.
- Type "use opus 5.5 high" to pick the model and effort. New work starts on a default you set in Settings (Auto, capable, High).
- When your plan's online use runs out (or reaches 99% for the week), your PC's own model takes over. One switch, on by default. If you turn it on, your API credits can take over first; answers that used them say so. Someone else's phone never uses your credits.
- Fresh start: when a conversation gets very long, a line warns you, then the task carries on in a new session from the project's hand-off, after the turn that is running ends. You can also ask for it from the task menu. Your messages come first and are never lost.
- A chat that needs your PC offers Project work right there, but only when the phone is paired to its owner's PC.
- Answers from memory say so and offer Look it up. Questions about recent studies and events are looked up. The AI is told never to claim a lookup or an action that did not happen.
- Look-ups read the top pages and answer from numbered sources. Open databases are searched beside the web, and a page that is gone is read from the Wayback Machine, which its source then says.
- Chats keep answering after a quiet spell: a link that looks open but carries nothing is made again, and the message your PC never got goes once more. A message is never done twice, and an edited message is never given the answer to its old words.
- A message never goes to a different answerer than the one shown when you typed it without a line saying so. If it would now go Online, it waits with a Send it again button.
- Back works the same everywhere: it goes to where you came from, then to Chat with the side menu, then out of the app. Typed words stay in the box when you come back and are forgotten once sent.
- The keyboard stays down when you open the app or come back to a screen, and goes away when you send.
- Words in any message can be selected and copied on their own.
- Every chat has the task chat's parts: times, read marks, thinking dots, New lines when scrolled up, and Find in chat.
- Projects, chats and tasks are listed newest first by your last message. On the PC window, Pin to top stays; moving rows by hand is gone.
- A task's top shows Back, its name, its project, its state and its tags in full. Its three dots start with its project and how full the conversation is.
- Whisper keeps nothing, on the phone or the PC, and opens empty.
- Tidier phone screens: no greeting by the hour, a chat's name said once, one Scan the code, one line under an answer.
- Fixed: coming back with the keyboard up no longer flashes the old picture; "stop using lists" is simply done instead of offering the Stop button; sessions found running elsewhere no longer add projects; tasks are named by their first words.

Known not working yet: Share into Mímir, paste and drag and drop are not fully checked. Usage savings, the speed check on a real phone (open under 1 second, a tap under a tenth of a second) and more search engines on the PC move to the next version. Still waiting: opening a project after the phone sits a long while can ask you to press Cancel first; the app sometimes cycles or flickers (cause not found yet); a reply can show twice; a long question to your PC's model can end with "didn't finish"; a "2 not done" card can show above your message before anything happened; Whisper screens still have the old look; Edit and Try again keeping the old answers is not checked; "Delete this chat" and the size of the Think first row are not re-checked.
