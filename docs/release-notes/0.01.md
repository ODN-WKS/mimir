# Mímir 0.01

The first release. Private by design.

- Ask anything from your PC or your phone. Chats stay with you.
- Pair your phone with your PC and keep them in step.
- Choose who answers: your PC or an online model.
- Updates are checked before they are installed.
- Labs: check that everything works, and report a problem in a few taps (nothing is sent unless you send it).
