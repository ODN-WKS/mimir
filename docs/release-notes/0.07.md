# Mímir 0.07

This version is about trust: what the screens say is true, and Mímir stays quiet unless it matters. It fixes 67 things found by reading every screen the way a new person would, and three things the owner found.

- Notifications you control. Settings › Notifications has "What to tell me": a task that needs you, a task that finished, sessions from other apps on your PC ("Only when they need me" unless you choose more), Mímir updates, and the "Working" notice. Each Mímir task alerts once, when it really finishes. Sessions you run in a terminal or another app no longer fill your phone with alerts. The read-aloud player goes away when it's done.
- A long conversation says so, in plain words: each reply re-reads all of it, so it uses more of your plan (or, on your PC's own model, gets slower). "Start fresh with a summary" carries on in a new task and keeps the old one. Never on a Whisper.
- Sessions running in a terminal or another app: the task says where it runs and what to do ("Still running in a terminal. Stop it to carry on here."), "running" ends within seconds of the session ending, and Carry on here works once it stops. Helper notices inside those sessions are one small line, not a wall of code.
- One true status everywhere: "Needs you" is one name, a task whose helper died says "Stopped unexpectedly" (with Continue) instead of Done, a Stop sent while your PC is away says it's waiting, and where project work goes (your PC or online) is the same on every screen before you send anything.
- When your PC's model isn't running, the chat says so with Try again, and the top of the window stops showing a green "This PC".
- Plainer screens on both sides: no file paths, technical model ids or milliseconds; two models with the same name are told apart; menus offer only what applies; pairing by paste explains itself; "PC away" is said once; search says when nothing is found.

Known not working yet: Edit in a task sends your words as a new message (truly replacing later replies comes later); a helper's report inside a followed session can't be opened yet; motion on older phones is not smooth yet.
