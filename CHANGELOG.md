# Changelog

Every Mímir version, newest first. Full notes for each are in `docs/release-notes/`.

## 0.11
Everything fixed since 0.10, put together: a status card in the chat with no model used, a press-and-hold menu to move, fold, archive or delete on the phone, a Skill choice beside Model and Effort, typed "use opus 5.5 high", the PC's own model (or your API credits, if you allow it) taking over when online use runs out, Fresh start when a conversation runs long, honest answers from memory, look-ups from numbered sources, chats that keep answering after a quiet spell and never do a message twice, and one Back that works the same everywhere. See `docs/release-notes/0.11.md`.

## 0.10
The phone's conversations, easier to follow and the same everywhere: one reply box with one model and effort chip, a grey bubble around every answer, a small Listen button, times and read receipts on every message with the answering model beside them, moving dots while the AI works, Recent activity in place of Updates, project tasks named by their start time, a slim checks line, and temperatures on Your PC. See `docs/release-notes/0.10.md`.

## 0.09
What got in the way on the phone, fixed: New chat is really new, every answer says who wrote it, look-ups that work and a PC model that starts when needed, Stop at the top with a confirm, a task menu with Archive and Delete, project rows you can archive or delete, your PC's numbers and Claude use on the phone, and quieter notifications. See `docs/release-notes/0.09.md`.

## 0.08
Usable for real work from the phone: a live sign of what each task is doing, photos and files in task replies, model and effort per reply, your PC's own tools in Mímir's sessions, Stop for read-aloud, the PC's processor, memory and graphics at the top of the window again, and quieter pop-ups. See `docs/release-notes/0.08.md`.

## 0.07
True screens and quiet notifications: 67 fixes from a screen-by-screen hunt plus three owner finds; notifications you control (one alert per task, nothing from other apps' sessions unless they need you); a plain notice when a conversation runs long, with Start fresh with a summary; sessions running elsewhere say where and how to carry on; one status everywhere. See `docs/release-notes/0.07.md`.

## 0.06
Your PC's own model first and Claude only an option; literal answers by default; hidden details removed from documents, recordings and zips, and files that can't be cleaned never go online; see what runs on your PC from the phone and stop it; Copy, Listen, Share, Try again and Edit in phone tasks; tables and code in answers; calmer, plainer screens on both sides. See `docs/release-notes/0.06.md`.

## 0.05
Alerts when a task finishes or needs you, even while the phone sleeps; Stop always there and really stops; honest task status; new tasks wait while the PC is away; New project in the side menu; pictures and files in tasks on the PC; a new bronze icon; PDF reader security fix. See `docs/release-notes/0.05.md`.

## 0.04
Photos always answered (another model steps in and says so), a new PC answers at once, right-click menus everywhere on the PC, per-chat drafts, one Try again on failed lookups, tablet menu stays open upright, clearer messages. See `docs/release-notes/0.04.md`.

## 0.03
Photos, documents and files from the phone go to the PC; nothing lost when the PC is away; every problem has a next step. See `docs/release-notes/0.03.md`.

## 0.02
First public release: pair once at the PC and use it from anywhere, each person on one PC kept apart, choose who answers, honest answers with sources, edit a message, app lock, signed updates for phone and PC. See `docs/release-notes/0.02.md`.

## 0.01
First release: chat from the PC or phone, pairing, choose who answers. See `docs/release-notes/0.01.md`.
